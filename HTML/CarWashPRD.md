# 🚗 Car Wash Booking App — Product Requirement Document (PRD)

## 🧭 Objective & Purpose

Build an app that allows users to schedule **car washing services** at their location, select service types, and make digital payments.

---

## 📌 Scope

### In Scope:
- Book a car wash
- Choose car type & service level
- Select time slot & address
- Online payment & notifications

### Out of Scope:
- Subscription/membership model
- Workshop-based bookings

---

## 🚀 Features

### P1:
- Booking form with calendar & map
- Payment gateway
- Booking history

### P2:
- Live location tracking of washer
- Offers & discount coupons

---

## 👥 User Stories

- 🧑‍🔧 As a **user**, I want to **book a car wash**, so I don’t need to visit the service center.
- 🧼 As a **washer**, I want to **see my appointments**, so I can plan my day.

---

## ⚙️ Tech Stack

- Frontend: React Native
- Backend: Node.js + Express
- DB: PostgreSQL
- Payment: Razorpay/UPI
- Notifications: Firebase
